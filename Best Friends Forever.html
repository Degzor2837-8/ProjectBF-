5 Rules of Friendship

1
Be honest.

Always tell the truth.

2
Listen carefully.

Pay attention to your friends.

3
Show kindness.

Help your friends when they need you.

4
Respect differences.

Everyone has their own opinions.

5
Forgive mistakes.

Talk about problems and try to understand each other.

3 Tips for a Strong Friendship

🎮Spend time together.
🙏Say sorry when you are wrong.
💪Support each other in difficult times.

Real-Life Situations

Situation 1: The Misunderstanding

Story: Emma thinks her best friend Sofia didn’t invite her to a walk on purpose. Emma gets offended and stops talking to her. Later, Sofia explains that her message didn’t send and she thought Emma was busy.

Moral: Don’t jump to conclusions. It’s better to talk first.

Situation 2: A Difficult Day

Story: Daniel gets a bad grade and feels upset. His friend Alex listens to him, helps him understand the hard tasks, and suggests preparing for the next test together.

Moral: A true friend doesn’t laugh at your failures — they support you.
